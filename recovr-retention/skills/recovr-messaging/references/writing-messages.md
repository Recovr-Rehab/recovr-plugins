# Writing member messages

A message works when it sounds like someone at the gym who knows the member wrote it.

## Rules

- **Short.** Two or three sentences for an SMS.
- **One true, specific detail:** the class they come to, how long they've been a member, the milestone they just hit. Use their real first name; never leave a placeholder such as `[name]`.
- **One small next step the member takes:** "The 6am is back on Thursday if you're keen." Neither the assistant nor this connection can book classes, so never offer to book them in.
- **Warm, no pressure:** no guilt, urgency or deadlines, and no stock lines like "we miss you". A discount comes last, if at all.
- **Australian English**, the gym's word for members, and the register of the gym's earlier messages in the thread.
- **Plain punctuation:** commas and full stops, no em dashes. No emoji unless the gym uses them.
- **Facts only from the records:** no guessed reasons, invented offers, or claims that something happened ("your hold is applied") unless the data shows it.

## Match the reason

| What the record shows | What the message does |
| --- | --- |
| Package expired while still attending | Makes renewing easy. Admin, not persuasion. |
| Visits faded over weeks | Asks an open question; you don't know the reason. |
| Stopped suddenly after regular visits | Warm and brief. No selling. |
| Injury or illness noted | Asks after them. No class pitch. |
| One or two visits, then nothing | Helps them get to a first proper session. |
| They wrote and nobody answered | Apologises for the wait and answers them. Nothing else. |
| Milestone or challenge progress | Names the number and the effort. |

## Examples

Faded member, 2 years, Tuesday spin regular:

> Hi Robin, it's Rowan from the studio. Haven't seen you at Tuesday spin for a few weeks, everything ok? No pressure, just checking in.

Milestone:

> Robin, that's 100 classes with us! Huge effort, see you at the 6am.

Not this (generic, guilt, booking promise, em dash):

> Hi [name] — we miss you! Don't let your progress slip. Want me to book you in this week?
